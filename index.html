Overview

What is PC Matic?

PC Matic is an American-made cybersecurity and antivirus software solution designed to protect personal computers, business networks, and connected devices from malware, ransomware, viruses, and other online threats. Developed by PC Pitstop, PC Matic has been a trusted name in the security industry since the early 2000s.

Unlike traditional antivirus programs that rely on blocklists of known threats, PC Matic uses a unique allowlist-based security model. This means only pre-approved, trusted applications are allowed to run on your system — an approach that offers powerful protection against zero-day attacks and previously unknown malware.

PC Matic's centralized account system lets you manage all protected devices from a single dashboard, monitor system health, run scans, and review security alerts — all from one convenient login portal. It is available for home users, businesses, and government organizations.

Login Guide

How to Login to PC Matic on Multiple Devices

PC Matic can be accessed through the desktop application installed on your PC, the web-based account portal, and the mobile app. Below are detailed step-by-step login instructions for each platform.

🌐
Logging In via the PC Matic Web Portal
  1. 1
    Open your preferred web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari) on any device.
  2. 2
    Navigate to the PC Matic website and click the Login or My Account link in the top navigation menu.
  3. 3
    Enter your registered email address in the email field on the login page and click Continue.
  4. 4
    Type in your account password and click Sign In to access your PC Matic dashboard.
  5. 5
    Once logged in, your account dashboard will display all registered devices, their security status, recent scan results, and subscription details.
🖥️
Logging In via the PC Matic Desktop App (Windows)
  1. 1
    Locate the PC Matic icon in your system tray (bottom-right of the taskbar) or on your desktop and double-click to open the application.
  2. 2
    If the app is not yet installed, download the installer from the official PC Matic website and run the setup wizard to install it on your Windows PC.
  3. 3
    On the PC Matic launch screen, click Sign In and enter your email address associated with your PC Matic subscription.
  4. 4
    Enter your password and click Log In. The application will authenticate your account and link this device to your subscription.
  5. 5
    After a successful login, PC Matic will run an initial scan and display your system health score and protection status on the main dashboard.
📱
Logging In on iPhone / iPad (iOS)
  1. 1
    Open the App Store on your iPhone or iPad, search for "PC Matic," and download the official app if it's not already installed.
  2. 2
    Launch the PC Matic app and tap Sign In on the welcome screen.
  3. 3
    Enter your PC Matic account email address and tap Next to proceed.
  4. 4
    Enter your password and tap Login. You may also enable Face ID or Touch ID for quicker future logins if prompted.
  5. 5
    Your mobile dashboard will load, showing device status, security alerts, and account management options.
🤖
Logging In on Android
  1. 1
    Open the Google Play Store, search for "PC Matic," and install the official app on your Android device.
  2. 2
    Open the app and tap Log In to Existing Account from the home screen.
  3. 3
    Type your registered email address and tap Continue.
  4. 4
    Enter your password and tap Sign In. Use fingerprint or face unlock if your device supports biometric authentication and you've enabled it.
  5. 5
    The app will connect to your PC Matic account and display your devices, scan history, and security alerts on the dashboard.
🏢
Logging In via PC Matic Business / MSP Admin Portal
  1. 1
    Open a web browser and go to the PC Matic business or MSP login portal (accessible from the PC Matic website under Business solutions).
  2. 2
    Enter your administrator email address and click Next.
  3. 3
    Enter your admin password and click Sign In. Multi-factor authentication may be required for administrator accounts.
  4. 4
    The admin console will open, giving you a full view of all managed endpoints, agent statuses, group policies, and security event logs.
  5. 5
    From here you can deploy agents, run remote scans, manage licenses, and configure security policies across your entire organization.
Support

Troubleshooting PC Matic Login Issues

Encountering a problem logging in? Here are the most common PC Matic login issues and how to fix them quickly:

🔑 Forgot Your Password?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email to receive a password reset link. Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

📧 Email Not Recognized?

Make sure you're using the exact email address used during registration. Check for typos, and try alternate email addresses if you have multiple accounts.

🔄 App Won't Open or Load?

Restart your computer and try launching PC Matic again. If the issue persists, try reinstalling the application. Make sure your Windows version meets the minimum requirements.

🌐 Web Portal Not Loading?

Clear your browser's cache and cookies, then try again. Try a different browser or disable browser extensions that might be blocking the page.

🚫 Account Locked or Suspended?

Multiple failed login attempts may temporarily lock your account. Wait 15–30 minutes before retrying. If your subscription has lapsed, renew it to restore full access.

📵 Not Receiving Verification Email?

Check your spam or junk mail folder. Add PC Matic's email domain to your safe-senders list. If the problem continues, try requesting the email again after a few minutes.

🖥️ Device Not Appearing in Dashboard?

Ensure PC Matic is installed and logged in on that device. Check that the device is connected to the internet. A manual sync from the app's settings menu can force the device to register.

⚠️ Subscription or License Error?

Log in to your web account portal and verify your subscription status under "My Account." If your license has expired or reached its device limit, you may need to upgrade or remove an old device.
